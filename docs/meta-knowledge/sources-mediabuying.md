## Outline

Source for all four domains and weightings: https://www.facebook.com/business/learn/certification/exams/410-101-exam

1. Campaign Activation — 38%
   - Set up campaigns in Ads Manager for business outcomes
   - Build and implement audiences
   - Establish brand safety controls
   - Configure Meta data sources
   - Navigate Meta Business Suite settings
2. Reporting — 20%
   - Prepare reports that track appropriate KPIs
   - Interpret campaign results through reporting
   - Set up measurement experiments
3. Creative Strategy — 16%
   - Apply creative best practices
   - Recall advertising policies
4. Campaign Optimization — 26%
   - Optimize campaigns per Meta best practices (delivery and performance)
   - Demonstrate knowledge of buying types, auction dynamics and reach settings

Note: the public page lists topics only at this level. A finer sub-topic list sits in the login-gated study guide and was not read (see Gaps).

Relevance for copywriters: domain 3 (Creative Strategy, 16%) maps directly to writing; parts of domain 1 (objectives, brand safety, comment controls) and domain 4 (auction, ad quality, learning phase) decide how copy is judged by the delivery system.

## Facts

Format: statement — source URL — domain. Numeric specs are quoted as shown on 2026-10-04 and CAN CHANGE; recheck the Ads Guide before hard-coding them.

### Objectives and campaign setup

- F1. Ads Manager has six simplified objectives: Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, App promotion, Sales. Meta tells advertisers to decide the business goal first, then pick the objective. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1438417719786914 — Campaign Activation
- F2. Awareness is for reaching the largest number of people most likely to remember the ad; suggested when a business is new or has changed its name. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1438417719786914 — Campaign Activation
- F3. Traffic sends people to a destination (website, app, Facebook Page, Instagram shop); Engagement finds people likely to message, engage with the ad or Page; Leads collects leads via messages, calls or sign-ups; Sales finds people likely to purchase (and can optimize for other actions such as add to cart). — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1438417719786914 — Campaign Activation
- F4. Old objectives map to the new ones with extra settings, e.g. Brand awareness -> Awareness with performance goal "Ad recall lift"; Video views -> Awareness (ThruPlay or 2-second continuous video views) or Engagement (video views); Conversions -> Engagement / Leads / Sales depending on conversion location. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1438417719786914 — Campaign Activation
- F5. A Special Ad Category must be declared at campaign creation for ads about social issues, elections or politics (authorization plus "Paid for by" disclaimer) and for employment, housing, or financial products and services (limited audience selection tools); ads may be rejected if the right category is not chosen. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/298000447747885 — Campaign Activation

### Auction, ad quality, optimization

- F6. The auction winner is the ad with the highest total value, built from three factors: bid, estimated action rates, and ad quality. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/430291176997542 — Campaign Optimization
- F7. Estimated action rates plus ad quality together measure ad relevance, so a more relevant ad can beat ads with higher bids. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/430291176997542 — Campaign Optimization
- F8. Meta states that clickbait and engagement bait do not improve ad performance. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/430291176997542 — Campaign Optimization / Creative Strategy
- F9. Ad quality draws on feedback from people viewing or hiding the ad and on low-quality attributes: withholding information, sensationalized language, engagement bait. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/303639570334185 — Campaign Optimization / Creative Strategy
- F10. Ad relevance diagnostics have three dimensions: quality ranking, engagement rate ranking, conversion rate ranking; quality ranking values run from "Above average" down to "Below average (bottom 10% of ads)", compared against ads competing for the same audience, last 35 days only. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/303639570334185 — Reporting
- F11. Lower-quality ads tend to cost more and get less distribution; repeatedly posting policy-violating or low-quality ads can lead the system to treat all ads from that Page, domain or ad account as lower quality. Landing pages count: low-quality attributes must also be absent from the post-click experience. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/423781975167984 — Campaign Optimization
- F12. Learning phase: an ad set usually exits after about 50 results in the week after its last significant edit; during learning, performance is less stable and CPA is usually higher. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/112167992830700 — Campaign Optimization
- F13. Learning-phase best practices: do not edit until the ad set exits learning, avoid unnecessary edits, avoid high ad volumes (combine similar ad sets), use realistic budgets. For copy teams this means: do not keep swapping ad text on a live ad set. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/112167992830700 — Campaign Optimization

### Ad copy fields and text

- F14. Meta's two text best practices: keep ad copy short (primary text 1-3 lines at most) and make what you want people to do clear at a glance — "the most important goal of your ad". — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/223409425500940 — Creative Strategy
- F15. Recommended length for most placements: primary text 125 characters, headline 40 characters, description 25 characters; longer text may be truncated, and truncation varies by placement and device. (Numeric; can change.) — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/223409425500940 — Creative Strategy
- F16. If ad text is left blank, text may be pulled from the destination URL and may not be editable; text of an existing Page post used as an ad cannot be edited in the ad. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/223409425500940 — Creative Strategy
- F17. Multiple text options can be entered for primary text, headline and description on single image/video ads; the system serves variations per person. Text generation can produce up to 5 variations of primary text and headline. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/223409425500940 — Creative Strategy
- F18. Placement asset customization lets copy differ by placement, e.g. shorter copy on Stories, more brand-led copy on Instagram. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/223409425500940 — Creative Strategy
- F19. Advantage+ creative enhancements (such as text improvements) may alter uploaded text; some are on by default and can be turned off. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/223409425500940 — Creative Strategy
- F20. Ads Guide, Facebook Feed image ad (traffic): ratio 4:5, resolution 1440 x 1800 px, primary text 50-150 characters, headline 27 characters, JPG or PNG, max 30 MB. (Numeric; can change. Differs from F15 because the Ads Guide is per placement.) — https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/facebook-feed/link-clicks — Creative Strategy

### Visual format, Reels, Stories, video

- F21. On mobile, creative should cover as much of the screen as possible. Feed: 1:1 and 4:5 supported (4:5 recommended for single image on Facebook Feed, 1:1 for Instagram Feed). Stories, Status and Reels: 9:16. Carousel: 1:1 or 4:5, kept consistent across cards. In-stream video: 16:9. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/103816146375741 — Creative Strategy
- F22. Text overlay best practices: clean modern font, large enough, contrasting color; do not obstruct the visual; do not pack in many messages because an ad usually has one call to action; stay inside the safe zone. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490 — Creative Strategy
- F23. Safe zone for 9:16 ads: keep top, bottom and side edges free of key elements, text and logos; if a Reels ad carries a disclaimer, leave the bottom 40% free. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490 — Creative Strategy
- F24. Ads Guide figure for the safe zone: leave about 14% at the top, 35% at the bottom and 6% on each side free of text and logos (Instagram Reels and Stories). (Numeric; can change.) — https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/instagram-reels — Creative Strategy
- F25. Instagram Stories video ad: 9:16, 1440 x 2560 px, primary text 125 characters, captions and sound "optional, but recommended", duration 1 second to 60 minutes; videos under 16 seconds play in full, longer ones may be split into Story cards. (Numeric; can change.) — https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-story — Creative Strategy
- F26. Reels ads: Meta's three-part recipe is vertical 9:16 video, with audio, with key messages in the safe zone; Meta reports such campaigns had 2x higher delivery to Reels and 34.5% lower cost per result than image ads. (Meta-reported figures.) — https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads — Creative Strategy
- F27. Mobile video ads: hook in the opening seconds with the most captivating element, show the brand early, keep it short (15 seconds or less), design for sound-off with text and graphics while rewarding sound-on, frame vertically (4:5 or 9:16). Meta reports captions raised view duration by 12% on average. (Older Meta article; treat numbers as historical.) — https://www.facebook.com/business/news/want-to-better-video-ads-for-mobile-well-show-you-how — Creative Strategy
- F28. Captions can be uploaded as an .srt file (named filename.[language]_[country].srt) or auto-generated; captions attach to the video, so they appear in every ad using that video. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1675722002698686 — Creative Strategy

### Organic posts, Live, distribution

- F29. Facebook ranks organic posts in four steps: inventory, signals, predictions, score. Predictions include likelihood to comment, spend time, share, find the post informative — and also likelihood that it is clickbait or links to a low-quality webpage, which count against it. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/718033381901819 — (supporting knowledge; closest domain: Creative Strategy)
- F30. Engagement bait = urging people to like, share, comment, tag or vote to boost reach. Four named types: react baiting, comment baiting (asking for specific words, numbers or emojis), share baiting, vote baiting. Posts and Pages using it get less distribution. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/259911614709806 — Creative Strategy
- F31. Clickbait is distinct: it prompts clicks by withholding crucial information or creating misleading expectations through exaggeration. Genuine requests for help, advice or recommendations are not treated as engagement bait. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/259911614709806 — Creative Strategy
- F32. Facebook Live guidance: announce ahead with a post or Scheduled Live, write a catchy description, give people time to join, answer viewers by name, pin a comment to steer the conversation, go live at least 10 minutes (up to 4 hours), signal the end. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/246791142703681 — (supporting knowledge; Creative Strategy)

### Comments and community management

- F33. On ads, admins can like and reply to comments (Facebook) and hide or report comments (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) from Ads Manager. Proactive filters — profanity filter on Facebook, Hidden Words on Instagram and Threads — are set at Page/profile level and extend to ads from that Page or profile. — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1129470964230971 — Campaign Activation (brand safety controls)

### Advertising policies that govern wording

- F34. Ad review starts automatically before an ad runs and is typically completed within 24 hours; it checks image, video, text, targeting and the landing page; ads can be re-reviewed and rejected at any time; advertisers can request a review. — https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/ — Creative Strategy (policies)
- F35. Personal attributes: ads must not assert or imply a person's race, ethnicity, religion, beliefs, age, sexual orientation, gender identity, disability, physical or mental health, financial status, voting status, union membership, criminal record or name. Meta's examples: "Depression counseling" is allowed; "Do you have diabetes?" and "Meet other seniors" are not. Rule of thumb: describe the product or service, not the reader. — https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/privacy-violations-personal-attributes/ — Creative Strategy (policies)
- F36. Health and wellness: no copy that creates negative self-perception (statements of inferiority about appearance, body parts or hygiene); no sensational or exaggerated claims or promises of specific outcomes in a set timeframe without qualifiers; no claims to cure incurable diseases; diet, weight loss and similar products must target 18+. — https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness/ — Creative Strategy (policies)
- F37. Prohibited commercial practices: ads must not use deceptive practices such as deceptive pricing, unauthorized endorsements, or guaranteed investment returns. — https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/fraud-scams/unacceptable-business-practices/ — Creative Strategy (policies)
- F38. Profanity: ads must not contain profanity, including obscured, misspelled or symbol-substituted forms and profane emojis or gestures. — https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/profanity/ — Creative Strategy (policies)
- F39. Intellectual property: ads must not infringe third-party copyright, trademark or other legal rights, or promote counterfeit goods. — https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/intellectual-property-infringement/third-party-infringement/ — Creative Strategy (policies)
- F40. Alcohol ads must follow local law and age targeting; they are prohibited outright in some countries and minimum ages vary by country. — https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/alcohol/ — Creative Strategy (policies)
- F41. Promotions (contests, giveaways, sweepstakes) run on a Page, group or event must: follow applicable law (official rules, terms, eligibility); include a complete release of Meta by each participant; acknowledge the promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by or associated with Meta; and must NOT require or reward sharing, reposting, tagging others or otherwise promoting the promotion. (Read on the zh-TW version of the page.) — https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/pages_groups_events — Creative Strategy (policies; organic)
- F42. Branded content (content featuring or influenced by a business partner for an exchange of value, including free gifts) may only be posted with the branded content tool, tagging the partner with their prior permission; some categories are prohibited (e.g. tobacco, weapons, multilevel marketing, payday loans) and some restricted (alcohol, subscription services, financial products, cosmetic procedures and weight loss). — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/221149188908254 — Creative Strategy (policies; organic)

Total facts: 42.
